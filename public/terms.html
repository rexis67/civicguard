Terms of Use

Last updated: 25 October 2026

CivicGuard is not an emergency service. If there is immediate danger, a crime in progress or a medical emergency, call your local emergency number. Do not use this site to request emergency help.

1. The service

CivicGuard is an open-source demonstration that lets residents report non-urgent municipal issues, view a public incident feed, and lets authorized staff triage and update reports. It is provided for evaluation and demonstration purposes. Reports may not reach the relevant municipal department, and the operator is not a government agency.

2. Acceptable use

  • Submit only issues you have observed or reasonably believe to be accurate.
  • Do not submit unlawful, harassing, discriminatory, defamatory or knowingly false content.
  • Do not upload malware, attempt to break authentication, scrape at abusive rates, or probe the service for vulnerabilities without permission.
  • Do not include personal data about other people, identity documents, or confidential information in a report or photo.

3. Accounts and authority access

You are responsible for keeping your password secure and for activity under your account. Authority accounts are invite-gated and may be revoked. Do not share invite codes or use authority tools for purposes unrelated to municipal incident response.

4. Your content

You keep ownership of the content you submit. By submitting it, you grant the operator a non-exclusive licence to store, display, process and share it as needed to operate and demonstrate the service, including sending report text and photos to the AI provider for triage. Reports and photos are public unless removed.

5. AI output

Category, severity, urgency, summaries and tags are generated automatically and may be wrong, incomplete or biased. They are decision support, not an official determination. Authority staff remain responsible for verifying reports and deciding on a response.

6. Availability and changes

The service is provided on an “as is” and “as available” basis, without warranties of any kind. Features, data and this deployment may change or be discontinued at any time, and demonstration data may be reset without notice.
