Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

CivicGuard is an open-source demonstration deployment. Please do not submit confidential information, personal identification documents or anything you would not want publicly visible. Reports and attached photos appear in the public incident feed.

What we collect

  • Report content: title, category, district, description and an optional photo.
  • Optional location: map coordinates you place or capture with browser geolocation. If you do not add a pin, no coordinates are stored. Pins are public on the incident map.
  • Account details: name, email address and a bcrypt password hash. Plain-text passwords are never stored.
  • Technical data: IP address and request metadata are processed transiently for rate limiting, abuse prevention and server logs.
  • Browser storage: a signed session token is kept in localStorage so you stay signed in. It is not a tracking cookie.

Why we use it

  • Display reports and their optional location pins in the public feed and the authority operations dashboard.
  • Run automated triage to classify, summarize and prioritize reports.
  • Verify accounts, authorize authority actions and prevent abuse.
  • Operate, debug and secure the service.

AI triage

When you submit a report, its description and optional photo are sent to Google's Gemini API for classification. The result (category, severity, urgency, summary and tags) is stored with the report. Google processes that data under its own API terms. Do not include sensitive personal information in a report or photo.

Cookies and analytics

CivicGuard does not set advertising or analytics cookies and does not load third-party trackers. Fonts are served from this deployment, not from a third-party font CDN. The only client-side storage is the authentication token described above, plus any data your browser caches as part of normal page loading. The incident map loads raster tiles directly from Esri; those requests expose your IP address to Esri but are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking. Browser geolocation is requested only when you press Use my location and is subject to your browser permission.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with the infrastructure needed to run the service: the hosting provider (Render), the AI provider (Google Gemini) and the map tile provider (Esri). We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Retention

Reports remain until an authority member or the operator deletes them. Account records remain until the account is removed. Demonstration deployments may be reset, which removes reports and accounts without notice. Backups and server logs are kept only as long as needed for operations.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, sessions use signed JWTs with an expiry, verification tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes, and authority actions are role-guarded. No system is perfectly secure; report suspected issues through the contact channel below.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy or deletion of your account and reports, or object to processing, by opening an issue at github.com/rexis67/civicguard/issues. Include the email address used for the account so the request can be verified. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority.

Children

CivicGuard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. Material changes will be reflected in the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or requests: CivicGuard issue tracker. For emergencies, contact your local emergency number instead — CivicGuard is not an emergency service.
